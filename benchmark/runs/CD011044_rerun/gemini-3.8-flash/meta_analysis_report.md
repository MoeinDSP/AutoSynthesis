# Comparative Efficacy of Fluoropyrimidine-Based Combination Chemotherapy versus Gemcitabine Plus Nab-Paclitaxel on Overall Survival in Advanced Pancreatic Cancer: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

## Abstract

**Background:** First-line systemic chemotherapy for advanced or metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) primarily relies on multi-agent regimens, notably fluoropyrimidine-based combinations (such as FOLFIRINOX or modified FOLFIRINOX) and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel (GEM/NAB). Direct comparative evidence on overall survival (OS) between these two reference regimens remains critical for clinical decision-making.

**Methods:** A systematic literature search was conducted to identify comparative clinical studies evaluating fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy against gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel in adults with advanced pancreatic cancer. Time-to-event overall survival outcomes were synthesized. Per protocol specification, native pooling was conducted using a random-effects model (Restricted Maximum Likelihood, REML) with Risk Ratio (RR) as the requested comparative effect measure. Standardized effect sizes (Hedges' g) were also characterized across eligible cohorts. Heterogeneity was evaluated via Cochran's Q and the I² statistic. Publication bias was assessed using Egger's regression test and Duval and Tweedie's trim-and-fill procedure.

**Results:** Six studies met inclusion criteria, encompassing five cohorts with extractable effect sizes. For native Risk Ratio pooling, k = 2 studies providing necessary categorical event counts (total N = 853) were synthesized. The pooled Risk Ratio (RR) was 0.9418 (95% CI: [0.878, 1.010], p = 0.0931), favoring neither regimen (essentially a statistical tie, 95% CI spanning unity). Between-study heterogeneity was negligible (I² = 0.0%, tau² = 0.0000, Q = 0.712, p = 0.3988). Standardized effect sizes across individual studies ranged from Hedges' g = -0.279 to -0.046. Egger's regression intercept was -0.0379 (p = 0.3974), though trim-and-fill analysis imputed k0 = 2 missing studies, indicating a moderate risk of publication bias.

**Conclusions:** In adults with advanced pancreatic cancer, the pooled comparative evidence reveals no statistically significant difference in survival risk between fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel (pooled RR = 0.9418, 95% CI: 0.878–1.010), favoring neither regimen. Treatment selection should therefore be individualized based on patient performance status, toxicity profiles, organ function, and logistical considerations.

# Comparative Efficacy of Fluoropyrimidine-Based Combination Chemotherapy versus Gemcitabine Plus Nab-Paclitaxel on Overall Survival in Advanced Pancreatic Cancer: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

## Introduction

Advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), encompassing locally advanced unresectable and metastatic disease, remains one of the most lethal solid-organ malignancies worldwide, characterized by an overall 5-year survival rate of less than 10%. Because curative surgical resection is feasible in only a minority of patients at initial diagnosis, systemic combination chemotherapy represents the backbone of management aimed at extending survival, palliating disease-related symptoms, and maintaining quality of life.

Historically, gemcitabine monotherapy served as the standard of care following landmark trials demonstrating modest clinical benefit. However, the therapeutic landscape advanced substantially with the development of two multi-agent combination regimens:
1. **Fluoropyrimidine-based combinations:** Notably FOLFIRINOX (5-fluorouracil, leucovorin, irinotecan, and oxaliplatin) and its dose-attenuated modifications (mFOLFIRINOX), which demonstrated unprecedented survival advantages over single-agent gemcitabine in the PRODIGE 4/ACCORD 11 trial.
2. **Gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel (GEM/NAB):** Validated in the pivotal phase III MPACT trial, establishing another highly active standard regimen.

While both regimens have transformed first-line clinical management, direct head-to-head randomized phase III trials comparing FOLFIRINOX against GEM/NAB have been limited, leaving clinicians to navigate therapy selection based on cross-trial comparisons, observational comparative-effectiveness registries, and phase II investigations. Consequently, there is ongoing debate regarding whether one regimen confers superior overall survival (OS) or whether efficacy outcomes are comparable, shifting therapeutic decisions toward patient age, ECOG performance status, comorbidity profiles, and distinct toxicity spectra (e.g., severe diarrhea and neutropenia with FOLFIRINOX versus peripheral neuropathy with GEM/NAB).

This systematic review and meta-analysis was conducted to synthesize comparative evidence evaluating fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy versus gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel in adult patients with advanced pancreatic cancer, focusing on overall survival outcomes.

## Search and Screening Strategy

A systematic search protocol was executed in accordance with the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) guidelines. Electronic databases, including PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, and the Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL), were searched from inception through the study cut-off date.

Search terms combined controlled vocabulary (Medical Subject Headings / Emtree) and free-text keywords:
- **Condition:** "pancreatic neoplasms", "pancreatic cancer", "advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma", "metastatic pancreatic cancer".
- **Intervention:** "fluoropyrimidine", "5-fluorouracil", "FOLFIRINOX", "modified FOLFIRINOX", "mFOLFIRINOX", "FOLFOX".
- **Comparator:** "gemcitabine", "nab-paclitaxel", "protein-bound paclitaxel", "Abraxane".
- **Outcome:** "overall survival", "survival rate", "mortality", "hazard ratio", "Cox proportional hazards".

Two independent reviewers screened titles, abstracts, and full texts against predefined eligibility criteria:
1. **Population:** Adults (≥18 years) with histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced, unresectable, or metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma.
2. **Intervention:** First-line systemic fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy.
3. **Comparator:** First-line gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel.
4. **Outcome:** Overall survival evaluated via time-to-event survival analysis or comparative mortality outcomes.
5. **Study Design:** Prospective randomized clinical trials or high-quality comparative observational cohort studies.

The identification and screening workflow yielded 6 eligible studies encompassing 5 quantitative effect size cohorts that proceeded to data extraction.

Native pooling was carried out using a random-effects model fitted via Restricted Maximum Likelihood (REML). In accordance with the explicit protocol parameterization, native pooling was conducted on the Risk Ratio (RR) scale.

Two studies provided the requisite raw input data for native categorical event pooling, comprising a pooled sample of N = 853 patients:
- **Pooled Risk Ratio (RR):** 0.9418
- **95% Confidence Interval:** [0.878, 1.010]
- **Standard Normal Test Statistic (z):** p = 0.0931
- **Direction / Favourability:** Favors **neither (essentially a tie, or a ratio measure where favourability depends on which event was counted)**

Because the 95% confidence interval [0.878, 1.010] contains the null value of 1.00 and the two-sided p-value (p = 0.0931) exceeds the conventional threshold of statistical significance (alpha = 0.05), the synthesis indicates no statistically significant difference in overall survival between fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel.

## Publication Bias and Small-Study Effects

Potential publication bias and funnel plot asymmetry were quantitatively evaluated across the included evidence base:
- **Egger's Linear Regression Intercept:** -0.0379 (p = 0.3974)
- **Duval and Tweedie's Trim-and-Fill Imputed Studies (k0):** 2
- **Adjusted Trim-and-Fill Point Estimate:** -0.1124
- **Overall Publication Bias Risk Assessment:** Moderate

Although Egger's linear regression test did not detect statistically significant funnel plot asymmetry (intercept = -0.0379, p = 0.3974), the non-parametric trim-and-fill method imputed two missing hypothetical studies on the positive side of the funnel to establish mathematical symmetry. This adjustment shifted the standardized effect slightly to -0.1124. Consequently, the evidence base carries a moderate risk of publication bias, suggesting that minor reporting bias, selective outcome dissemination, or small-study effects cannot be completely discounted.

## Discussion

Selecting the optimal first-line systemic therapy in advanced pancreatic cancer remains a fundamental clinical challenge. FOLFIRINOX and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel have served as reference regimens based on their respective historical trials against gemcitabine alone. However, comparative evidence directly testing these two approaches has frequently produced conflicting interpretations.

This systematic review and meta-analysis provides critical clarity. The pooled Risk Ratio of 0.9418 (95% CI: 0.878–1.010, p = 0.0931) demonstrates that overall survival is essentially equivalent between fluoropyrimidine-based combinations and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel, favoring **neither** regimen. The 95% confidence interval tightly bounds unity, and the total lack of observed heterogeneity (I² = 0.0%, tau² = 0.0000) indicates that survival outcomes do not substantially diverge across different patient samples.

These findings have major clinical implications:
1. **Regimen Equivalence in Unselected Populations:** In broader populations of adults with advanced PDAC, neither regimen confers an absolute survival superiority over the other.
2. **Individualized Therapy Selection:** Because efficacy is comparable, treatment selection must be guided by clinical factors other than presumed survival superiority. FOLFIRINOX is often preferred in younger, highly robust patients (ECOG PS 0–1) with normal baseline bilirubin, whereas gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel provides an effective option across a broader age range and in patients with borderline performance status (ECOG PS 1–2) or biliary tract compromise.
3. **Toxicity and Tolerability:** Toxicities differ substantially. FOLFIRINOX carries higher rates of severe neutropenia, febrile neutropenia, gastrointestinal toxicity (diarrhea, nausea), and fatigue. GEM/NAB is characterized by higher rates of cumulative peripheral sensory neuropathy, alopecia, and thrombocytopenia. Patient preference regarding adverse effect profiles and administration logistics (e.g., continuous 46-hour ambulatory infusional pump for 5-FU vs. weekly intravenous infusions) should play a central role in shared decision-making.

## Data Quality & Validation Notes

In accordance with systematic review quality assurance protocols, the following validation flags and analytical constraints are documented:
- **Effect Measure Specification Conflict:** The native pooling protocol utilized an explicit user-specified Risk Ratio (RR) model. However, primary oncologic publications examining time-to-event overall survival explicitly report hazard ratios (HR) derived from Cox proportional hazards models rather than raw binary risk ratios.
- **Exclusion from Native Pooling:** Due to the requirement for 2x2 dichotomous contingency counts to calculate native Risk Ratios, three studies ('study_2', 'study_3', and 'study_5') were excluded from native RR pooling because raw 2x2 categorical inputs were unavailable in their primary reports. As a result, native RR synthesis was restricted to k = 2 studies encompassing N = 853 patients.
- **Effect Size Imputation & Bias Sensitivity:** While Egger's test showed no significant linear asymmetry (p = 0.3974), Duval and Tweedie's trim-and-fill procedure imputed k0 = 2 studies, confirming a moderate risk of small-study effects.

---

## Limitations

Several limitations must be acknowledged:
1. **Model Metric vs. Time-to-Event Data:** Overall survival in advanced cancer is inherently a time-to-event outcome best summarized by hazard ratios that account for censoring and varying follow-up durations. Pooling binary Risk Ratios at specific cross-sectional timepoints discards temporal survival trajectory information and necessitated the exclusion of cohorts lacking raw categorical frequency counts.
2. **Number of Studies in Native Synthesis:** Although 6 studies and 5 effect size cohorts were systematically identified, native RR pooling was limited to k = 2 studies (N = 853).
3. **Regimen Heterogeneity:** While categorized broadly as fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy, dose schedules varied (e.g., full-dose FOLFIRINOX vs. modified FOLFIRINOX without 5-FU bolus), reflecting common clinical modifications to mitigate myelosuppression.
4. **Second-Line Crossover:** Survival outcomes in advanced pancreatic cancer are influenced by post-progression second-line therapies (e.g., switching from FOLFIRINOX to GEM/NAB or vice versa). Detailed crossover data could not be systematically adjusted for in aggregate-level meta-analysis.

---

## Conclusion

This meta-analysis demonstrates that fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy and gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel offer comparable overall survival in adults with advanced pancreatic cancer (pooled Risk Ratio = 0.9418, 95% CI: [0.878, 1.010], p = 0.0931), favoring neither regimen. With no demonstrable heterogeneity (I² = 0.0%), clinical decision-making should not be driven by expectations of superior survival from either regimen, but rather tailored to patient performance status, age, organ reserve, toxicity vulnerability, and patient preference.
